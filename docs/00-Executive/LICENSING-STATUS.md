# Licensing Status

## Current status

GeoGuide AI prioritizes Open Source technologies for the MVP to reduce licensing
costs, enable self-hosting, avoid vendor lock-in, preserve portability, and make
the operational pilot economically viable.

This technology strategy does not determine the product licensing or
intellectual-property strategy. Product licensing remains under review after
validation of the operational pilot.

## Rules

- `LICENSE` is the current legal file.
- No AI agent may replace or modify `LICENSE` without explicit owner approval.
- Third-party licenses, attribution requirements, and data licenses must always
  be respected.
- Historical statements that propose a product Open Source license are retained
  for traceability and require licensing review; they are not a legal decision.
