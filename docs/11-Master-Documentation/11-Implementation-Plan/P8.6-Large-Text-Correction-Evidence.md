## Finding and correction

Before the correction, the three actions (`Cancelar`, `Restablecer`, `Aplicar`) were equal-width `Expanded` children in one horizontal `Row`. At large text scale, the narrow button constraints forced the longer labels to wrap and visually fragment. The category tiles were not affected.

The action area now uses a full-width vertical button stack inside a scrollable sheet. Labels remain complete at the user's configured text scale, touch targets retain standard Material semantics, and no font-size clamp, truncation, or hidden text was introduced.
