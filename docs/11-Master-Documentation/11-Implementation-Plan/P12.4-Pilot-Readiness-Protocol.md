# P12.4 Pilot Readiness Protocol

## Status and boundary

```ini
P12.4 = PREPARATION_AUTHORIZED
P12.5 = NOT_AUTHORIZED
P12_FIELD_EXECUTION = NOT_AUTHORIZED
P12_CONTRACT = APPROVED_FROZEN
P11_BACKUP_01 = OPEN_DEFERRED
```

P12.4 defines readiness for a controlled real-road pilot. It does not execute a road test, certify navigation safety, or add product capabilities.

## Objective and corridor

The candidate corridor is Lima–Canta–Obrajillo using the existing GraphHopper/API route and curated P3 POI data. P12.2 evidence recorded a successful HTTP 200 LineString response of 100701.323 m and 6388.947 s with five existing POIs. A later stationary device session displayed a coherent 97.7 km route and five POIs; that observation is retained as device evidence with manual-coordinate tolerance.

## Roles and safety

- Driver: focuses exclusively on driving and never touches the device while moving.
- Passenger/observer: operates the app, records bounded technical observations and can abort the test.
- Test owner: confirms the build, route, evidence manifest and stop conditions before departure.

GPS Follow Mode must be activated while stationary before movement. Driving must operate without interaction with secondary controls. The test stops immediately if the app distracts, displays misleading navigation guidance, loses the route context, or any participant considers conditions unsafe. Driving UI is not certified turn-by-turn navigation.

## Evidence and retention

Collect only pass/fail results, build/device identifiers without full serials, bounded timestamps, non-sensitive error codes and manual observations. Do not retain trajectories, continuous precise location, audio or personal travel history. Delete transient technical evidence after the approved short retention period and document deletion without exposing coordinates.

## Abort and rollback

Abort before or during the pilot on unsafe conditions, unexpected permissions, TLS/API mismatch, route loss, unbounded location collection, app crash, material distraction or any Critical/High finding. Do not alter backend, API, DB, infrastructure, contracts or device data to force a pass. Restore the previous app build only through an explicitly reviewed, non-destructive procedure.

## Human gate

P12.5 requires a separate human authorization naming the route, date/window, participants, observer, APK hash, privacy protocol and abort conditions. Until that authorization exists, the project remains at P12.4 readiness preparation.
